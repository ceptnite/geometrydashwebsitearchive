On this page you can find information about the of Geometry Dash!

  • Stereo Madness is the first level of Geometry Dash, and the first level with an Easy difficulty. Stereo Madness introduces the cube form with sequences involving blocks and spikes with simple jumping patterns. The ship form is also introduced with sequences involving spacious manoeuvres around obstacles. Ship sequences take place from 30% to 48% and from 85% to 100%.

  • Back On Track is the second level of Geometry Dash, and the second level with an Easy difficulty. Back On Track introduces the yellow jump pad in the ways of both function and potential hazard, leading to more varied jump manoeuvres. A single ship sequence takes place from 50% to 66%.

  • Polargeist is the third level of Geometry Dash, and the first level with a Normal difficulty. Polargeist introduces the yellow jump ring, beginning to extend the degree of jumping challenges, particularly during the second cube sequence. A single ship sequence takes place from 34% to 47%.

  • Dry Out is the fourth level of Geometry Dash, and the second level with a Normal difficulty. Dry Out introduces the gravity portal with an antigravity cube segment. Vertical obstructions occur more often, requiring stricter movement that restrains against unnecessary jumping. An antigravity cube segment takes place from 34% to 51% and a single ship sequence from 67% to 84%.

  • Base After Base is the fifth level of Geometry Dash, and the first level with a Hard difficulty. It maintains a similar difficulty to Dry Out. A single ship sequence takes place from 53% to 69% and an antigravity cube segment from 71% to 85%.

  • Can't Let Go is the sixth level of Geometry Dash, and the second level with a Hard difficulty. Can't Let Go increases the precision and timing required for jump manoeuvres including spike arrangements with spikes positioned just clear of overhead spikes. The antigravity segment initiating the second cube sequence involves jump rings and elevates the difficulty so far experienced. A single ship sequence takes place from 57% to 70% and an antigravity cube segment from 70% to 86%.

  • Jumper is the seventh level of Geometry Dash, and the first level with a Harder difficulty. Jumper involves tighter manoeuvres and increases the frequency of antigravity segments, which is also experienced with the ship for the first time during its first sequence. Ship sequences take place from 25% to 37% and from 62% to 75%.

  • Time Machine is the eighth level of Geometry Dash, and the second level with a Harder difficulty. Time Machine introduces the mirror portal. Difficulty is increased as triple spike arrangements are frequent and antigravity segments become routine, also occurring during the final ship sequence.
  • A cube sequence takes place from 0% to 35%, while under the effects of the mirror portal from 29%;
  • A ship sequence from 35% to 43%, with the mirror portal's effects sustained;
  • A cube sequence from 43% to 92%, with the mirror portal's effects ceasing at 57%, continuing at 71%, ceasing at 78%, continuing at 85% and ceasing at 89%;
  • And a ship sequence from 92% to 100%.

  • Cycles is the ninth level of Geometry Dash, and the third level with a Harder difficulty. Cycles introduces the ball form with a light use of pads, rings and gravity portals. Misleading jump pads and rings leading to hazards as well as triple spike arrangements are prominent during the first cube sequence. The single ship sequence involves rapidly alternating gravity.
  • A cube sequence takes place from 0% to 36%;
  • A ball sequence from 36% to 54%;
  • A ship sequence from 54% to 71%;
  • A cube transition sequence from 71% to 74%;
  • And a ball sequence from 74% to 100%, while under the effects of the mirror portal from 74%, ceasing at 82% and continuing at 91%.

  • xStep is the tenth level of Geometry Dash and Geometry Dash Lite and the first level with an Insane difficulty.
  • A cube sequence takes place from 0% to 8%;
  • A ball sequence from 9% to 18%;
  • A ship sequence from 18% to 27%;
  • A cube sequence from 27% to 36%;
  • A ship sequence from 36% to 54%;
  • A cube sequence from 54% to 72%;
  • A ball sequence from 72% to 90%;
  • And a ship sequence from 90% to 100%.

  • Clutterfunk is the eleventh level of Geometry Dash, and the second level with an Insane difficulty.
  • A cube sequence takes place from 0% to 29%, while under the effects of the mirror portal from 4% to 7%, and under the effects of the size portal from 14% to 29%;
  • A ship sequence from 29% to 36%;
  • A ball sequence from 36% to 53%, while under the effects of the size portal from 36%;
  • A ship sequence from 53% to 67%, with the size portal's effects ceasing at 60%;
  • And a cube sequence from 67% to 100%, while under the effects of the size portal from 67%, ceasing at 71%, continuing at 74%, ceasing at 77% and continuing at 82%.

  • Theory of Everything is the twelfth level of Geometry Dash, and the third level with an Insane difficulty. Theory of Everything introduces the magenta jump pad and magenta jump ring, which are frequently used in combination with other pads and rings during cube and ball sequences. The UFO form is also introduced and with segments involving the size portal. Hazardous alternate paths appear regularly during ship and UFO sequences, with the correct paths indicated by arrow pulsators.
  • A cube sequence takes place from 0% to 8%;
  • A ship sequence from 8% to 16%, while under the effects of the size portal from 10%, ceasing at 13%, continuing at 15% and ceasing at 16%;
  • A cube sequence from 16% to 26%, while under the effects of the size portal from 22% to 25%;
  • A UFO sequence from 26% to 34%;
  • A cube sequence from 34% to 44%;
  • A ball sequence from 44% to 53%, while under the effects of the mirror portal from 44% to 51%;
  • A ship sequence from 53% to 57%, while under the effects of the size portal from 53% to 57%;
  • A cube sequence from 57% to 64%;
  • A UFO sequence from 64% to 81%, while under the effects of the size portal from 72% to 80%, and under the effects of the mirror portal from 72% to 81%;
  • A cube sequence from 81% to 91%;
  • A ball sequence from 91% to 95%, while under the effects of the size portal from 91%;
  • And a ship sequence from 95% to 100%, with the size portal's effects sustained.
